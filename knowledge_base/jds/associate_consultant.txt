About this role

The Associate Consultant serves as a key advisor and relationship manager for clients, supporting consulting engagements, account management, and program delivery. In this role, you will build strong client relationships, understand business needs, provide recommendations, and coordinate with internal teams to ensure successful outcomes.

Working with leading private-sector organizations and government entities, you will gain hands-on experience in consulting, stakeholder management, and client servicing. The role offers a fast-paced learning environment, providing exposure and professional growth that can significantly accelerate your career development.



Key responsibilities

Understand client objectives, identify business challenges, and provide recommendations and solutions to support desired outcomes.
Manage the day-to-day delivery of client engagements, programs, and accounts, ensuring timely execution and high-quality results.
Establish and manage strategic partnerships with both local and international entities to drive growth. 
Responsible for onboarding and orienting clients, explaining all the details about the benefits and requirements, and offering recommendations based on the understanding of the market.
Follow up on the execution of the required activities from the operations side, tracks deadlines, and provides support to the operations team whenever needed.
Promote Setup.sa’s services among clients to achieve sales, while ensuring a thorough understanding of our services, and a seamless execution through providing any necessary information.
Identify opportunities to strengthen client partnerships, improve service delivery, and support the growth of existing accounts. 
Manage client relationships by maintaining continuous communication to understand their needs, gather information and provide updates.
Analyze and interpret data, and develop quantitative and qualitative analysis reports.
Support the development of various types of reports.
Perform various procedural tasks and services at the government agencies locations.
Build presentations, and arrange client meetings.
Build and maintain databases of all the information of the clients, and create comprehensive documentation.
